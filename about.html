About Jay

Jay Tyler Sappington was a student at the University of Central Florida, a devoted guitar player and songwriter, a passionate member of Chabad of Greater Daytona, a fisherman extraordinaire, and a beloved grandson, son, brother, neighbor, student, and friend.

Jay worked part-time at Express in the Florida Mall. For eight years, he played roller hockey for the illustrious Daytona Thunder. He went on great adventures with his siblings and inspired many friends and relations with his love of life, amazing observations of the goodness in people, and this wonderful world G-d has given us.

Jay brought a love of life and a profound appreciation for the goodness in people to everything he did. He left behind a legacy of music, warmth, and inspiration for his family and friends.

This site serves as an archive to preserve his songs, lyrics, and memory.

About This Redesign

This website is a modern redesign and archival preservation project created by Gittel Rosenblatt. The goal of this project was to honor Jay Sappington’s memory by improving the site’s mobile responsiveness, audio access, and overall visual layout while keeping the original content completely intact.

You can view the original 2005 website layout and archive at sappingtonmusic.com.
